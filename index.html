Privacy Policy

Royal Checkers is designed to let you enjoy the game without creating an account or signing in. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Royal Checkers and describes information handling, local storage, internet connectivity, advertising, third-party services, and the choices available to users.

1 Information We Collect

Royal Checkers does not require you to provide a name, email address, password, or account information to play. The app settings and statistics are described as being stored on your device. The app may also process technical information through its advertising or other integrated services, depending on the services enabled and your device settings.

2 Local Storage

Your game preferences, such as board theme, sound settings, animation speed, and other settings, are intended to make your experience more convenient. The app states that statistics and settings are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove locally saved information.

3 Internet Connection

Royal Checkers requires an internet connection for its online functionality. Network connections may also be used by integrated third-party services, including advertising services, where applicable.

4 Advertising

Royal Checkers includes advertising functionality. Advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, advertising interactions, approximate location derived from network information, or diagnostic data, depending on the provider, your device settings, and applicable consent choices. The precise information processed depends on the advertising SDK and its configuration.

5 Advertising Privacy Choices

Where available, you can open Ad Privacy Options from the Settings screen to review or manage the advertising privacy choices presented by the app. You can also review your Android device settings for available advertising and privacy controls.

6 Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services to provide advertising or other functionality. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Their data practices are governed by the services actually integrated into the app.

7 Data Sharing

Royal Checkers does not require an account to play. Information processed by third-party services, such as advertising providers, may be handled by those providers in accordance with their policies and the choices available to you. Please review the applicable provider policies to understand their data practices.

8 Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by the app and its service providers. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9 Children’s Privacy

Royal Checkers does not ask players to create accounts or submit personal information to play. However, advertising and other third-party services may have their own age-related data practices and requirements. The app should be used in accordance with applicable age restrictions and local laws.

10 Your Choices

You can change the game settings at any time. You can also use the available advertising privacy controls where provided. You may remove locally stored app data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app, although this may also remove saved settings and statistics.

11 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, its features, or its integrated services change. Any revised policy should be made available with the updated app or through the developer’s official privacy policy publication.

12 Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Royal Checkers handles information, contact the app developer using the contact details provided on the app’s Google Play Store listing.
